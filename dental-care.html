FAQ

Common dental questions, answered.

Are dental implants painful?
The procedure is done under local anesthesia and most patients report only mild discomfort afterwards, well managed with standard medication.
How long does a root canal take?
Most cases are completed in 1–2 sittings, depending on the tooth and infection level.
Do you treat children?
Yes — we offer pediatric-friendly consultations with gentle pacing and clear communication.
How often should I get a dental check-up?
Every 6 months for most adults; we'll personalise this after your first visit.
Do you offer EMI / payment plans?
For larger treatments like implants, structured payment options can be discussed transparently.
